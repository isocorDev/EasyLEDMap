EasyLEDMap

Map your LEDs from a photo

Add a photo of the strips, then trace the path the data takes. EasyLEDMap counts and places the pixels along it and writes the map for WLED, FastLED or TouchDesigner.

You can also drop photos or a .ledmap file anywhere on this page. Nothing is uploaded: everything stays on this computer.
